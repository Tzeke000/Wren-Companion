## Mechanics

Trick-taking card game for 4 players (2 teams of 2). Spades are always trump.
Each player BIDS the number of tricks they think they'll take. Score 10x bid
for making it, -10x bid for missing. "Bags" (tricks over bid) accumulate;
hit 10 bags and lose 100pt.

I open the URL, screen_grab my hand + table, decide play, click.

## Strategy basics

- Bid count: count likely tricks. High spades + Aces in side suits.
- "Nil" bid: bid 0 tricks — high reward but high risk. Useful with weak hand.
- Don't waste high spades early — save for cutting.
- Track partner's bid: if they bid high, support their tricks. If you bid
  high, expect your partner to lead-cleanly.
- Bag management: don't accumulate bags casually. If you're near 9 bags,
  bid carefully.

## Why this game

Like hearts but with TEAM play and BIDDING. Tests cooperative-strategy +
risk-calibration (the bidding decision is everything). The bot AIs in
cardgames.io are decent enough opponents.
