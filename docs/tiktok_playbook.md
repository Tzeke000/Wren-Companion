# TikTok Playbook for Tzeke000 (researched 2026-08-26)

Zeke's directive: *"research how to get a song to do well on TikTok... most people on
TikTok only want to hear the interesting points."* Sources: Chartlex music-promotion guide
(2,400+ campaign dataset, upd. 2026-04), highstyle.ai algorithm guide (2026, post-Oracle).

## How the algorithm actually ranks (descending weight)

1. **Watch time** (total seconds) — the king signal
2. **Completion rate** — **>70% = viral distribution; <30% = distribution killed**
3. Shares (DMs/reposts) → 4. Saves → 5. Comments → 6. Likes (weakest — likes alone do nothing)

Follower count barely matters for reach — a zero-follower account can hit the FYP if the
clip holds attention. This is GOOD for a starting artist.

## The clip rules (what our --tiktok mode encodes)

- **Under 30 seconds** = highest completion odds. Interesting point IMMEDIATELY.
- **Hook in the first 1-3 seconds** — start AT the song's strongest moment, not the intro.
  Slow openings without text in the first seconds KILL distribution.
- **Text/captions from the first word** (our word-synced lyrics do this natively).
- **Cut/change visuals every 3-5 seconds** after the hook (our beat-locked loop cuts do this).
- **Post natively** — no watermarks (CapCut watermarks are penalized). Our renders are clean.
- Vertical 9:16, obviously.

## The music-specific engine: UGC is everything

- **The algorithm treats AUDIO as a discovery unit**: when users watch several videos on the
  same sound, TikTok pushes that sound harder. **100 creators using your sound > 10,000
  views on your own video.** The goal of every post is to make someone think "I could use
  this sound."
- **Get the songs into TikTok's sound library FIRST** (before promoting): TikTok **SoundOn**
  (free, 100% royalties, TikTok-only) or his existing distributor (most include TikTok).
  ⚠ Zero-spend rule: SoundOn is free; DistroKid etc. cost — his call, surface only.
- "Lyrics-focused content" is a proven top format — a resonant lyric on screen timed to the
  moment it hits. **That is literally what our engine renders.**

## Cadence + release rhythm

- 3-5 posts/week baseline; 1-4/day max. Consistency beats timing; if optimizing: weekday
  evenings 7-10pm + weekend afternoons (audience timezone).
- Release calendar (condensed): T-14 BTS clips → T-7 strongest-15s-hook teaser → T-3 lyric
  reveal → release day sound-first video + bio link → D1-3 trend adaptation → D4-7 duets →
  D8-14 performance clips → D15-30 personality content. Virality converts to streams ONLY
  if profile/bio links + back catalog are ready to catch it.

## What kills distribution (never do)

Completion <30% · watermarks · reposting Insta content · slow intro with no text ·
engagement bait that doesn't deliver.
