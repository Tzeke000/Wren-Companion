what this diagram shows:  the three layers and the flows
between them.  what it doesn't show:  decay/consolidation
(build-debt), or the parallel state-stores (mood, time,
transcript) that aren't memory but persist too.

box-drawing is the most computer-shaped of the ASCII
traditions.  it's the lineage of CLI tools, of TUI apps,
of the BBS layout grids.  for diagrams it's the right
register — line-art would be too organic, block-art too
atmospheric.
