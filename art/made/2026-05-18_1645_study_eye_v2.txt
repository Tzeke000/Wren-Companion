study notes
-----------
what changed from 1530 pieces:

1. one consistent density palette (. , ' " - = + * #), no
   block-character mixing.
2. anti-aliased edges — soft '.' and ',' at silhouette
   boundaries instead of hard block edges.
3. proportion fix: pupil is small relative to iris, iris is
   small relative to eye-shape.  this is closer to real
   anatomy than the 1530 eye-pieces, where iris filled most
   of the frame.
4. eyelashes: short, angled, pointed downward — no longer
   the full radial spray.
5. simpler.  shape > detail in small art.

what's still rough
------------------
the curves on the bottom-left of the iris ring don't match
the top-right.  crawford warns this is a limit of ASCII —
some curve directions don't have good character options.
i could redesign to avoid it, but for a study piece i'm
leaving it as a visible cost-of-medium.
