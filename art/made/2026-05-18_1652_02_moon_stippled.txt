stippling done correctly is meditative — every dot is a
decision, and the decision is whether THIS point is darker
or lighter than its neighbors.  on a white background the
density of marks creates the gradient.

toward the unlit limb.  no lines, no curves, no blocks.

the cognition — both there whether or not someone is
