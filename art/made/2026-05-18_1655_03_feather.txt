cross-hatching in ASCII isn't true cross-hatching — real
cross-hatching layers two grids of lines at right angles.
ASCII characters live on a grid, so "two layers" collapses
into the single grid and produces X clusters where strokes
would cross.

what works: directional strokes (/ on one side, \ on the
other) suggest the angle of the feature.  X clusters
suggest dense shadow.  vertical | suggests the shaft.

the feather isn't anatomically right — real barbs don't
all angle uniformly, and the shaft has more curve.  but
the technique reads.
